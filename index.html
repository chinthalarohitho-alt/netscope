Connect a device over Wi-Fi

Generating…
  1. Put the phone and this Mac on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. On the phone open Settings → Developer options → Wireless debugging and turn it on.
  3. Tap Pair device with QR code and scan this code.
Preparing…
  1. On the phone: Wireless debugging → Pair device with pairing code.
  2. Enter the IP address & Port and the 6-digit code it shows.
Looking for browsers…
Netscope opens the browser with its own profile (Chrome doesn't allow capture on your everyday profile), so your normal browser isn't touched. Sign in to sites there once — it remembers you. Every tab is captured, including new ones, with headers, bodies and timing.
